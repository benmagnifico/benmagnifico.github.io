I am deeply honored to receive the **Dean's Honors List Award 2025/2026** from the **Faculty of Science and Technology (FST), University of Macau** — recognized as the **FST MSc Graduate Dean's Honors List: The Best of the Department of Computer and Information Science**. This is the highest honor the department confers upon a graduating M.Sc. student, and I am profoundly grateful to my advisors, **Prof. Chengzhong Xu** and **Prof. Li Li**, and to everyone who supported me throughout my studies. 🎉

### Recognized by KAIST

This news was also featured by the **Cho Chun Shik Graduate School of Mobility at KAIST**, which I will join as a Ph.D. student in Fall 2026 under the supervision of **Dr. Heye Huang** in the **ACES Lab**. The full announcement is available here: [Jingguang Li Receives FST MSc Graduate Dean's Honors List Award from the University of Macau](https://mo.kaist.ac.kr/en/news_event/jingguang-li-receives-fst-msc-graduate-deans-honors-list-award-from-the-university-of-macau/). I am truly grateful for their warm welcome, and I look forward to expanding my research into **Safety-Critical Embodied System** at KAIST. 🚀

### The Ceremony

The award was presented at the **UM FST Celebration for "One to Three, and Beyond" — Glory of the Faculty**, during the *Presentation of the FST MSc Graduate Dean's Honors List – The Best of Department*.
